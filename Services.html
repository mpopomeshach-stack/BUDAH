Experience the Living History of Lesotho

Our Services & Experiences

Discover heritage, hospitality and outdoor activities in one memorable setting.

Guided Tours & Heritage

Cave & Art Tours

Guided walks to the massive Clarens sandstone overhang, featuring prehistoric San rock art and Stone Age archaeological deposits.

Historical Information

Learn about the site's history as a shelter for early people and a royal stopover for King Moshoeshoe I.

Visitor Centre / Museum

Displays and exhibits showcasing traditional Basotho culture and local history.

Accommodation & Food

Accommodation

Thatched-roof stone houses and huts featuring mountain views, upper-level bedrooms and modern comforts.

Dining & Bar

On-site bar, room service, and lunch or dinner options available upon request.

Outdoor & Nature Activities

Hiking & Nature Walks

Short trails and stone-paved pathways through the four-hectare nature reserve, with a natural water feature and stream.

Bird Watching

Opportunities to spot local birdlife and native high-altitude plants, including the spiral aloe.

Stopover & Travel Base

A convenient stopover for travellers heading towards Afriski Mountain Resort.
